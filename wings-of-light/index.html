A Scrollytelling Study

Wings of Light

Follow the bird. Scroll slowly — the scroll is the flight.

I  ·  The Descent

أَوَلَمْ يَرَوْا إِلَى الطَّيْرِ فَوْقَهُمْ صَافَّاتٍ وَيَقْبِضْنَ

Do they not see the birds above them — spreading their wings, and folding them in? None holds them aloft but the Most Merciful.

Qur’an 67:19 — placeholder verse

II  ·  Landfall

The clouds part like curtains drawn back, and the earth rises slowly to meet the traveler — green, after the long blue.

III  ·  The Rivers

جَنَّاتٍ تَجْرِي مِن تَحْتِهَا الْأَنْهَارُ

…gardens beneath which rivers flow… The bird banks low, and the water keeps pace beneath its shadow.

the recurring promise — placeholder verse

IV  ·  The Weave

Through arch after arch the path threads onward — shade, then light, then shade again — and the river keeps its promise alongside.

V  ·  The Return

And when the journey has shown what it was sent to show, the sky calls its traveler home.

يَا أَيَّتُهَا النَّفْسُ الْمُطْمَئِنَّةُ ارْجِعِي إِلَىٰ رَبِّكِ رَاضِيَةً مَّرْضِيَّةً

O soul at peace — return to your Lord, well-pleased and pleasing.

Qur’an 89:27–28 — placeholder verse  ·  end of study 01
