A Scrollytelling Study · II

Wings of Light

You are flying just behind the bird. Scroll — the scroll is the flight.

I  ·  The Descent

أَوَلَمْ يَرَوْا إِلَى الطَّيْرِ فَوْقَهُمْ صَافَّاتٍ وَيَقْبِضْنَ

Do they not see the birds above them — spreading their wings, and folding them in? None holds them aloft but the Most Merciful.

Qur’an 67:19 — placeholder verse

II  ·  Landfall

The cloud deck breaks around you, and the earth opens below — a green country, stitched with silver water.

III  ·  The River

جَنَّاتٍ تَجْرِي مِن تَحْتِهَا الْأَنْهَارُ

…gardens beneath which rivers flow… The bird holds the river’s line, and you hold the bird’s.

the recurring promise — placeholder verse

IV  ·  The Weave

Arch after arch rises out of the distance and swallows you whole — shade, then light, then shade again.

V  ·  The Return

Then the river lets you go, the ground falls away, and the sky calls its traveler home.

يَا أَيَّتُهَا النَّفْسُ الْمُطْمَئِنَّةُ ارْجِعِي إِلَىٰ رَبِّكِ رَاضِيَةً مَّرْضِيَّةً

O soul at peace — return to your Lord, well-pleased and pleasing.

Qur’an 89:27–28 — placeholder verse · end of study 02
